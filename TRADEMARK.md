# Tún trademark policy

**Tún™** and the **Tún mark™** are unregistered trademarks. Write ™, never ®. Nothing is
registered. The mark is the framed word "Tún" whose accent is a green check, plus the square
mark and the one-colour version derived from it. The files are in
[`plugins/tun/assets/`](plugins/tun/assets/).

## The licences do not cover the name or the mark

- The code licence, the GNU AGPL ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)), is a copyright licence. It gives
  rights to the code. It gives no right to the name or the mark.
- The [Tún Artefact Exception](COPYING.ARTEFACT-EXCEPTION) gives a permission for generated
  artefacts. It gives no right to the name or the mark either.

## Allowed without asking

- **Say what is true.** "Works with Tún", "built with Tún", "based on Tún", "a fork of Tún".
- **Keep the Origin Notice.** The Exception requires every generated artefact to keep an
  Origin Notice that names Tún. Keeping it, and keeping a visible "Made with Tún", is allowed
  use of the name.
- **Pass on Tún as it is.** A copy, or a fork that only tracks upstream and changes none of
  its code, may keep the name.

## Not allowed

- **A changed fork under the same name.** A fork that changes the code must use another name.
  It may say that it is based on Tún.
- **Suggested endorsement.** Do not use the name or the mark in a way that suggests the Tún
  project made, endorses, certifies or supports your product, service or fork.
- **The mark as your own logo.** Do not use the mark, or something confusingly like it, to
  identify your own product.

## Who holds the mark

The mark follows the project. The project belongs to the authors in [`AUTHORS.md`](AUTHORS.md),
and no employer holds rights in it ([`docs/17-licensing.md`](docs/17-licensing.md), "Status",
item 1). This policy says how the name and the mark may be used.

This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Searches in trademark registries are still open:
see [`docs/18-naming.md`](docs/18-naming.md).

# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related docs:** link to deeper background or empirical measurements

## Context

What problem, constraint, or measured fact forced a decision? State what was measured versus assumed.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly enough that a contributor or agent knows what to build and what to refuse.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what this unlocks or simplifies.
- **Trade-offs:** what this rules out or costs.
- **When to revisit:** the specific empirical change that would reopen this decision.

## Enforcement

Which automated test, verifier, or CI check proves this decision still holds? If none exists yet, say so explicitly and name the gap.
